Smart business insights

Evidence-based observations from the dataset.

01 / CATEGORY

Electronics needs attention

Monthly revenue declined from ₹4.57 Cr in January to ₹2.97 Cr in June. Investigate demand, stock and pricing.

02 / GROWTH

Apparel gained revenue

Apparel revenue increased from around ₹1.00 Cr in January to ₹1.35 Cr in June.

03 / TIMING

Saturday led weekdays

Saturday had the highest average daily revenue; Monday had the lowest in this dataset.

04 / MONTHLY

January was strongest

January recorded the highest total monthly revenue and June the lowest.

05 / DISCOUNTS

Check discount effectiveness

Average units per transaction stayed close to 2.57–2.60 across discount levels.

06 / NEXT STEP

Validate before investing

Compare revenue with costs, stock availability and margins before making business decisions.
