Key Findings

Important observations from the analysis.

01. Electronics Declined

Revenue fell from ₹4.57 Cr in January to ₹2.97 Cr in June, a decrease of 35.02%.

02. Apparel Grew

Revenue increased from ₹1.00 Cr in January to ₹1.35 Cr in June.

03. Grocery Stability

Grocery revenue stayed comparatively stable during the six-month period.

04. Saturday Sales

Saturday recorded the highest average daily revenue, while Monday was the lowest.

05. Monthly Trend

January had the highest monthly revenue, while June had the lowest.

06. Discount Patterns

Average units sold remained around 2.57–2.60 across discount levels.

Business Recommendations

Suggested next steps based on observed data.

Review Electronics

Check product demand, stock availability, pricing and supplier changes to understand the decline.

Study Apparel Growth

Identify which products and stores contributed to the revenue increase.

Test Discounts

Compare discounts with profit margins before deciding whether promotions are effective.

Analyze Weekdays

Investigate lower Monday sales and test targeted promotions.

Data limitation: The dataset does not include product costs, profit margins, stock availability or marketing spend. Therefore, revenue trends alone cannot establish profitability or explain the causes of sales changes.
